`maplibre_native` is used through the [maplibre](https://pub.dev/packages/maplibre)
package and is not added to an app by itself. See the
[documentation](https://maplibre.org/flutter-maplibre/docs/getting-started/setup/)
to get started and the
[example app](https://github.com/maplibre/flutter-maplibre/tree/main/examples)
for complete examples.
